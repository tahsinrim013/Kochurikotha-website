Your Simple Planting Guide

Ready to begin? Here are some general steps to help you care for your seed.

🪴

01. Prepare the Soil

Choose a suitable pot or planting space and prepare loose, suitable soil.

🌱

02. Plant the Seed

Place the seed carefully at the recommended depth for that seed.

💧

03. Water Gently

Water according to the seed's needs. Avoid overwatering.

☀️

04. Provide Proper Care

Provide suitable sunlight and continue caring for your plant.

🌸

05. Enjoy the Growth

Be patient and enjoy watching your plant grow.

Note: Different seeds may have different requirements. Always follow any specific instructions provided with your seed.

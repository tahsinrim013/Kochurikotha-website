Our Green Mission

Kochurikotha is a youth-led social initiative exploring how water hyacinth and used paper can be transformed into useful, more environmentally conscious materials.

We also encourage people to care for plants and make thoughtful everyday choices for a greener tomorrow.

Your Simple Planting Guide

Start your planting journey with these general steps and enjoy caring for your plant.

01 · Prepare the Soil
Choose a small pot or suitable place with loose, well-draining soil.

02 · Plant the Seed
Place the seed in the soil and cover it lightly. The right planting depth can vary by seed.

03 · Water Gently
Moisten the soil gently. Keep it suitably moist, not waterlogged.

04 · Give It Care
Place it where it can receive suitable light and check the soil regularly.

05 · Watch It Grow
Be patient, care for your plant, and enjoy every stage of its growth.

A Little Reminder: Seed needs are different. If your seed package includes specific planting instructions, always follow those instructions first.
